# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of **Airride for Forza Horizon 3**, on the [FH3 Mod Platform](https://github.com/Levilente/FH3_ModPlatform) 1.0.0.

- **Garage > Upgrades > Platform and Handling > Airride** (Stock / Airride, 3 000 CR), through the platform's upgrade bridge, stored per owned car, with its own icon.
- Below 20 km/h, hold **L3** for a second to lower the car smoothly; hold it again, or go faster than 20 km/h, to raise it.
- **Tuning > Springs > Airride > Drop**: 1 to 10 cm per car, in 0.1 cm steps (the row is only there on cars with the Airride).
- Extra negative camber per centimetre of drop, so the wheels tuck under the arches.
- The garage's showroom car sits lower while the cursor is on the Airride stage; the back button ends the preview.
- Only the picture is lowered by default (the car drives as before); `Visual=0` lowers the suspension instead.
- Only the player's own cars: showcase cars, loaners and AI cars get none.
- Settings in `fh3-mods\Airride.ini`; update checks through the platform; a quiet log (only problems) unless `Log=1`.
